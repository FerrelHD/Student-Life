# Privacy Policy — Student Life

_Last updated: 2026-07-27_

Student Life ("the app") is a personal academic/finance tracker. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

## What we collect

When you create an account, the app stores the following in your own private Supabase database row:

- **Account**: your email address and password (handled by Supabase Auth; we never see or store your raw password).
- **Profile**: display name, university, GPA, role/major, avatar, and app preferences (language, dark mode, notification settings).
- **Missions**: academic tasks you add — title, course, due date, priority, tag, XP reward, completion status.
- **Transactions**: income/expense entries you log — title, category, amount, date.
- **Savings goals**: a title and target/saved amount if you set one.
- **Notifications**: in-app notification records generated by the app.
- **Badge unlocks**: which achievement badges you've earned.

## How it's stored and protected

All of the above lives in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database with Row Level Security enabled on every table — each account can only ever read or write its own rows. No data is shared between users.

## Third-party services

- **Supabase** hosts the database and handles authentication. See [Supabase's privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy) for how they process data on our behalf.
- **Google Gemini API**: the optional Daily Quiz feature sends your app's current language setting and university name (if set) to Google's Gemini API, via a server-side function, to generate a quiz question. No other profile data, email, or personal identifiers are sent. If this call fails, the app falls back to a local offline question bank.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
- We don't track you across other apps or websites.
- We don't collect analytics or crash reports from third parties.

## Your data

You can edit or delete your missions, transactions, and profile details at any time from within the app. To delete your account entirely, contact us at the email below and we'll remove your account and all associated rows.
